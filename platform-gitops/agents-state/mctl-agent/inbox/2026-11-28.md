### 3. go-module-supply-chain-checks: Harden Go module supply chain in CI
**Category:** security
**Impact:** 3
**Effort:** 2
**Rationale:** Reports of malicious Go modules justify cheap checks: `go mod verify`, readonly module mode, review of new dependencies in PRs. Complements (does not duplicate) govulncheck-ci-gate, which covers known vulns only. CI-only, no runtime impact on `labs`.
**Source:** malicious Go modules report, 25 Nov.
